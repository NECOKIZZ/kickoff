## Video direction

Dark, glassmorphic, futuristic. Near-black canvas (#05060A) with deep-purple nebula
gradients; every surface is frosted glass (backdrop blur, 1px white-24% rim, top
highlight, soft inner glow). Brand purple #7B62F6 = the pick / the player; brand
green #00C805 = payout / proximity. Neon bloom on accents, persistent fine film
grain + faint scanlines, chromatic-aberration on glitch beats. Camera always
drifting (slow 3D parallax), never static. Clash Display for display type, Inter
for UI/mono-ish labels, JetBrains-style monospace for the terminal.
